What data do we collect?

You directly provide 300sf with most of the data we collect. We collect data and process data when you:

  • Register an account.
  • Create sightings, labels, checklists and more.

How will we use your data?

300sf collects your data so that we can:

  • Manage your account.

How do we store your data?

300sf securely stores your data at Digital Ocean's data center in Amsterdam, The Netherlands, EU.

300sf will retain your data for a duration of one year from your last sign-in date. After this predetermined period has elapsed, your data will be subject to deletion. We will notify you via email prior to the scheduled deletion to provide you with an opportunity to log in and prevent the removal of your data.

What are your data protection rights?

300sf would like to make sure you are fully aware of all of your data protection rights. Every user is entitled to the following:

The right to access – You have the right to request 300sf for copies of your personal data.

The right to rectification – You have the right to request that 300sf correct any information you believe is inaccurate. You also have the right to request 300sf to complete the information you believe is incomplete.

The right to erasure – You have the right to request that 300sf erase your personal data, under certain conditions.

The right to restrict processing – You have the right to request that 300sf restrict the processing of your personal data, under certain conditions.

The right to object to processing – You have the right to object to 300sf's processing of your personal data, under certain conditions.

The right to data portability – You have the right to request that 300sf transfer the data that we have collected to another organization, or directly to you, under certain conditions.

If you make a request, we have one month to respond to you. If you would like to exercise any of these rights, please contact us at our email: support@300sf.se

Cookies

Cookies are text files placed on your computer to collect standard Internet log information and visitor behavior information.

For further information, visit allaboutcookies.org.

How do we use cookies?

We do not use cookies or any other tracking technologies to collect personal information about our visitors. We do not collect or store any personally identifiable information automatically when you browse our website.

What types of cookies do we use?

None.

How to manage cookies

You can set your browser not to accept cookies, and the above website tells you how to remove cookies from your browser.

Privacy policies of other websites

The 300sf website contains links to other websites. Our privacy policy applies only to our website, so if you click on a link to another website, you should read their privacy policy.

Changes to our privacy policy

300sf keeps its privacy policy under regular review and places any updates on this web page. This privacy policy was last updated on 31 October 2023.

How to contact us

If you have any questions about 300sf's privacy policy, the data we hold on you, or you would like to exercise one of your data protection rights, please do not hesitate to contact us.

Email us at: support@300sf.se

How to contact the appropriate authority

Should you wish to report a complaint or if you feel that 300sf has not addressed your concern in a satisfactory manner, you may contact the Information Commissioner's Office, Datainspektionen.
