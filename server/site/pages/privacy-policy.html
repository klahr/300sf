Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data 300sf (the website and app at 300sf.se and its API) handles, why, and what you can do about it.

Who is responsible

AW Entertainment AB, Sweden, is the controller of your personal data. Contact: support@300sf.se.

What we collect, and why

DataWhyLegal basis

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or track you across other websites.

Who can see your data

  • Only you: your email address, your sightings with their positions and comments, your labels and your private checklists.
  • Other participants in a competition you join: your display name, username, portrait, and how many species you have and when you reached them.
  • Anyone with the link: the standings of public competitions, and checklists you share for comparison.
  • Our administrators: account details, when needed to run the Service and help you.

Subcontractors and other services

We use these subcontractors (processors) to run the Service, under data processing agreements:

  • DigitalOcean: servers and database, in Amsterdam, the Netherlands (EU).
  • Mailjet: sends our emails (EU).

Some parts of the app load content directly from other services. Your browser then sends them your IP address, as any website visit does:

  • OpenStreetMap (OpenStreetMap Foundation, United Kingdom): map tiles.
  • Wikipedia and Wikimedia Commons (Wikimedia Foundation, USA): species photos and articles.
  • xeno-canto (Netherlands): bird song recordings.
  • Stripe (Stripe Payments Europe, Ireland): only on the donation page, where Stripe handles the payment. Stripe is responsible for the payment data under its own privacy policy.

The United Kingdom has an EU adequacy decision. For the USA, transfers are covered by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep your data

  • Your account and everything in it are kept until you delete your account.
  • When you delete your account (in the app, confirmed by email), it is closed at once and permanently deleted after 7 days, so a mistake can still be undone by contacting us.
  • Sightings you delete are removed at once. Archived sightings and checklists are kept until you delete them.
  • Deleted data can remain in database backups until those backups are replaced.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • access your data, and take it with you: export your sightings and account data from the app, or ask us;
  • correct your data: most of it you can edit in the app;
  • delete your data: delete sightings, lists or your whole account in the app;
  • restrict or object to our processing, under certain conditions;
  • withdraw consent, such as access to your position, at any time.

Email support@300sf.se for anything you cannot do in the app. We answer within one month.

Complaints

If you think we handle your data wrongly, please contact us first. You can also complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (Integritetsskyddsmyndigheten, IMY), imy.se, or the data protection authority where you live.

Changes to this policy

We update this policy when the Service changes. We will tell you about important changes by email or in the app.
